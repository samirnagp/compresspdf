Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Website: https://www.easypdf.website

Privacy-First Guarantee: EasyPDF performs PDF and JPEG compression directly inside your web browser's local memory (RAM). Your documents and images are never permanently saved, archived, viewed, or sold by us.

At EasyPDF (accessible from https://www.easypdf.website), safeguarding the privacy of our visitors and their documents is our primary commitment. This Privacy Policy explains what types of information are collected and recorded by EasyPDF, how we handle uploaded or processed files, and your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. File Handling & Document Security

  • Client-Side Browser Processing: Our primary PDF and JPEG compression tools execute locally on your device using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly/JavaScript libraries. Your files remain on your device throughout compression.
  • Ephemeral Server API Processing: If a file is processed through our fallback compression API, it is handled strictly in volatile temporary memory (RAM) solely to generate the compressed output and is immediately purged once the download response completes.
  • No Permanent Storage or Indexing: We do not maintain databases of user documents, extract text/metadata from your files, or make user files accessible to search engines or third parties.

2. Information We Collect Automatically (Log Files)

Like most standard websites and cloud hosting providers (such as Render and Cloudflare), EasyPDF follows standard procedures for using server and security log files. These logs do not contain your compressed files and are used strictly for security, DDoS mitigation, and performance diagnostics. Information logged may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized or truncated where supported)
  • Browser type, device category, and operating system
  • Date and time stamps of requests
  • Referring/exit pages and HTTP status codes

3. Cookies, Web Beacons & Google AdSense

EasyPDF uses cookies and similar browser storage mechanisms to remember user preferences, measure aggregate site traffic, and display advertisements.

  • Google AdSense & Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their prior visits to www.easypdf.website or other websites on the internet.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Managing Cookies in Your Browser: You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your individual browser settings.

4. GDPR Data Protection Rights (EEA & UK Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to the following data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of any personal data we hold about you (note: we do not store user files or require account registration).
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under applicable conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You have the right to object to or request restrictions on how your data is processed, including opting out of non-essential analytics or ad personalization cookies.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request the transfer of data we have collected to another organization or directly to you.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@easypdf.website. We will respond within 30 days.

5. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know & Access: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data a business has collected about consumers.
  • Right to Delete: Request the deletion of any personal data collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: EasyPDF does not sell your personal information or uploaded documents. Where third-party advertising cookies constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under CPRA, you may opt out via Google Ads Settings or by enabling a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal in your browser.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA privacy rights.

6. Children's Information

Protecting children while using the internet is important to us. EasyPDF does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has provided personal information through our contact channels, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.
