Fast, Zero-Server Document & Image Compression

EasyPDF gives you instantaneous file size reduction without risking file privacy. Unlike conventional online compressors that upload your contracts, IDs, or personal photos to cloud servers, this tool executes entirely within your browser's sandboxed environment.

Using client-side JavaScript streams and HTML5 Canvas engines, your files are compressed in local system memory. Your confidential records, sensitive photos, and PDF files remain solely on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files ever stored or viewed?

No. No network transfer occurs during the compression process. Everything is parsed, optimized, and saved directly within your browser runtime.

Which formats are supported?

You can switch between PDF documents and standard JPEG/JPG images using the switch tabs at the top.

Is there any file size limit?

There are no arbitrary site limits. Because processing runs on your local machine, maximum capacity depends entirely on your available device memory.
