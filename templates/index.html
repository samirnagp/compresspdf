Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert PNG, WebP, or other image formats to JPEG?

Yes. Switch to the "Convert & Compress Image to JPEG" tab and select any PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF, or JPG picture. Transparent backgrounds are automatically filled with crisp white before exporting as JPEG.

How does PDF compression to 100KB or 10KB work?

The tool renders the pages using HTML5 Canvas, dynamically scales image assets and page resolution, and packages the optimized pages into a compact PDF file.

Are my files safe and private?

Yes. EasyPDF runs client-side inside your browser. No files are transferred to external cloud servers.
