Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Core Privacy Principle: Zero Retention

At EasyPDF (accessible from easypdf.website), your file confidentiality is our primary priority. We believe online utilities should not retain, profile, or sell personal documents.

2. How Your Files Are Processed

  • Browser-First Execution: In supported browsers, document rendering, quality scaling, and multi-pass compression are executed locally inside your web browser using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. In this mode, your documents never travel across the internet.
  • Automated Fallback Processing: When fallback processing is required (e.g. for low-memory devices or deep rasterization), files are transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection, processed in isolated temporary memory buffers, and immediately purged upon download completion.
  • No Long-Term Storage: We do not store your files on persistent disks, databases, or external object stores.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user account registration, email sign-ups, phone numbers, or credit card information. We never inspect, read, index, or train AI models on your uploaded PDF contents.

4. Cookies and Analytics

EasyPDF may use standard, privacy-friendly server logs strictly for network performance and DDOS mitigation. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or sell personal browsing metrics.

5. Data Security

All network communication with easypdf.website is secured using modern TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data security, please contact us at support@easypdf.website.
