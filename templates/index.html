Compress PDF & Images to Exact Size (10KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB)

Job application forms, college admissions, and government portals frequently mandate strict file size caps—such as photos under 10KB or PDF documents under 100KB. Manually resizing documents until they meet these constraints can be tedious.

EasyPDF performs adaptive rasterization and binary-search compression directly in your browser. All operations run directly in your device RAM without uploading files to remote servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does PDF compression to 100KB or 10KB work?

The tool renders the pages using HTML5 Canvas, dynamically scales image assets and page resolution, and packages the optimized pages into a compact PDF file.

Are my files safe and private?

Yes. EasyPDF runs client-side inside your browser. No files are transferred to external cloud servers.
