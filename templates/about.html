About EasyPDF

Fast, Privacy-First PDF & Image Compression for Everyday Needs

EasyPDF (https://www.easypdf.website) was created to solve a common frustration: shrinking PDF documents and JPEG photos to exact file size limits without sacrificing privacy, installing bloated apps, or dealing with hidden paywalls.

Our Core Mission: Deliver instant, exact-size document and image compression directly inside your web browser—keeping your sensitive files 100% private on your own device.

Why We Built EasyPDF

Students, job seekers, and professionals regularly face strict upload requirements on online application portals, university admission forms, and government recruitment systems—such as passport photos under 10 KB or 50 KB and scanned certificates under 100 KB or 200 KB.

Most online compressors require uploading your personal IDs, marksheets, and photos to remote cloud servers and only offer generic "low/medium/high" compression sliders that miss exact KB limits. EasyPDF solves both problems with target-size presets and client-side browser processing.

How EasyPDF Works

  • 100% Client-Side Execution: Using modern HTML5 Canvas, pdf.js, and pdf-lib, your device's own browser performs adaptive scaling and stream optimization locally in RAM.
  • Exact Target Size Presets: One-click presets for 10 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, and 200 KB automatically calibrate resolution and JPEG quality to fit strict form limits.
  • Mobile-First Speed: Lightweight inline vector graphics and lazy-loaded engines ensure fast performance even on mobile data connections.
