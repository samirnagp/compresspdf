Compress Files to Exact Size (10KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB)

Job application forms, college admissions, and government recruitment portals frequently mandate strict file size caps—such as photos under 10KB or documents under 100KB. Manually resizing and re-exporting images until they meet these constraints can be tedious.

EasyPDF automatically performs binary search optimization in your browser to meet your selected target size threshold without exceeding the maximum limit or compromising readability. All operations run directly in your device RAM without external server transmissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reduce my photo to under 10KB or 50KB?

Switch to the JPEG tab, upload your photo, and click the 10 KB or 50 KB preset. The tool balances dimensions and JPEG quality steps to bring the file below your chosen limit.

Are my files safe and private?

Yes. EasyPDF runs client-side inside your browser sandbox. None of your documents or photos are transferred across the network or saved on remote servers.

Can all PDFs be compressed to 10KB?

PDF documents containing heavy high-resolution scanned pages or multiple sheets may reach a physical compression limit. The tool strips metadata and compacts streams to the lowest achievable footprint.
